Two layers, two blind spots

Doctrine fires on explicit rules — radar radiating under EMCON ALPHA, speed past the patrol ceiling, corridor departure. It is auditable and it decides alone.

The behaviour layer compares the last five moments against 300 calibrated nominal windows and flags what resembles none of them. Watch frames 21–22: it contains the divergence two frames before any hard limit is crossed. Watch the EMCON breach and it stays quiet throughout — a 25 kW spike barely moves the sentence, so doctrine is what catches that one. Neither layer is sufficient.

Why containment outlasts the breach

After each anomaly ends the unit stays contained for four more frames. That is not a false alarm — the five-frame window still remembers the violation, so the unit's recent history is still abnormal even though its current telemetry is clean.

Containment lifts exactly when the breach falls out of the window. On the timeline these read as the pale amber tail after each red block.
